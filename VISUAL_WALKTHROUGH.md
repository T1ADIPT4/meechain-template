## 🎯 Color Scheme

**Primary Colors:**
- Background Gradient: `#667eea` → `#764ba2` (Purple gradient)
- Success: `#4CAF50` (Green)
- Warning: `#FF9800` (Orange)
- Error: `#F44336` (Red)

**Priority Colors:**
- High: `#ff5252` 🔴
- Medium: `#ffa726` 🟡
- Low: `#66bb6a` 🟢

**Text Colors:**
- White: `#ffffff`
- Semi-transparent: `rgba(255,255,255,0.9)`
- Dim: `rgba(255,255,255,0.7)`

## 🎨 Design Elements

**Cards:**
- Background: `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`
- Backdrop filter: `blur(10px)`
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`
- Border radius: `20px`

**Buttons:**
- Primary: `#4CAF50` with `50px` radius
- Secondary: `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)` with border
- Hover: Scale transform and bg change

**Progress Bars:**
- Container: `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`
- Fill: `#4CAF50`
- Height: `8-12px`
- Animated: `transition: width 0.3s`

## ✨ Interactions

**Hover Effects:**
- Cards: `translateY(-5px)` or `translateX(5px)`
- Buttons: `scale(1.05)`
- Background: Lighter opacity

**Click Effects:**
- Checkbox: Instant check/uncheck
- Task: Toggle completion
- Board: Navigate to detail

**Animations:**
- Progress bars: Smooth width animation
- Page transitions: Fade in
- Hover: 0.2s transitions

---

**Visual Design**: Modern, Clean, User-Friendly ✨
